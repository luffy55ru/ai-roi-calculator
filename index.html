How many words are in 1 Million LLM API tokens? ＋

In English text, 1,000 tokens equal approximately 750 words (or 1 word ≈ 1.33 tokens). Therefore, 1 Million tokens equals roughly 750,000 words—equivalent to about 10 full-length books.
