Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is Submagic better than OpusClip and CapCut Pro for short-form captions?

Yes, for creators publishing talking-head videos on TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts. Submagic specializes in Alex Hormozi-style animated word-by-word captions, auto-emojis, AI-selected B-roll overlays, auto sound effects, and zoom cuts across 48+ languages in under 60 seconds. OpusClip is tailored primarily for repurposing long podcasts into bite-sized clips, while CapCut Pro is a timeline video editor whose auto-captions require extensive manual keyframing and styling to match viral standards.

How much does Submagic cost compared to OpusClip in 2026?

Submagic starts at $16/month (Starter tier for up to 20 videos) and $39/month for Pro (up to 60 videos with full 4K export and auto B-roll). In contrast, OpusClip starts at approximately $29/month for Pro based on video processing credits (around 150-200 upload minutes per month). CapCut Pro costs $9.99/month per seat, but requires significantly more manual labor hours for caption animation and asset placement.

Can I use Submagic alongside CapCut or Premiere Pro?

Absolutely. Many top creators cut their raw footage in CapCut or Premiere, then export the clean 60-second clip into Submagic to automatically generate dynamic viral captions, punch-ins, sound effects, and B-roll in under a minute, cutting overall post-production time by over 90%.
