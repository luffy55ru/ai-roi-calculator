Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about pricing, CRM automation, accuracy, and enterprise storage.

How does Fireflies.ai pricing compare to Otter.ai Business in 2026? +

Fireflies.ai offers a Pro plan at $10/seat/month and a Business tier at $19/seat/month. Otter.ai Business starts at $20/seat/month when paid annually or $30/seat/month paid monthly. A 10-person team switching to Fireflies saves $1,200 to $2,400 annually in subscription fees alone while unlocking superior CRM and language capabilities.

Which tool has better CRM integration for sales and customer success? +

Fireflies.ai leads the industry with automated two-way integrations into HubSpot, Salesforce, Close, Pipedrive, and Zoho CRM. It attaches audio recordings, timestamped notes, and AI action items directly to corresponding contacts and opportunities. Otter.ai restricts direct CRM integration to Salesforce on its expensive Enterprise plan.

What are the transcription accuracy and language differences? +

Fireflies.ai supports over 100+ languages and regional accents with industry-leading 95%+ accuracy and custom industry vocabulary libraries. Otter.ai is primarily tailored for English speech, making Fireflies the definitive winner for multi-national, remote, or bilingual organizations.

Are there meeting duration or storage caps? +

Fireflies.ai provides unlimited storage on Pro and Business plans, allowing your team to archive thousands of hours of historical meetings without paying overage fees. Otter.ai caps conversation length at 90 minutes per meeting and enforces monthly minute ceilings (typically 1,200 to 6,000 minutes/month).

How do teams use CustomGPT.ai alongside Fireflies meeting notes? +

By connecting Fireflies.ai transcripts to CustomGPT.ai, organizations build an internal AI employee that remembers every executive decision, client objection, and product requirement discussed on calls. Employees can ask questions like "What discount did we promise Acme Corp last month?" and get instant answers with cited meeting timestamps.
